Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Trader Vaults collects the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Wallet address (public blockchain identifier)
  • Username and display name (if provided)
  • X (Twitter) handle (if linked for verification)
  • Email address (if provided)

Usage Data

  • Platform interactions and trading activity
  • Vault participation and investment amounts
  • Performance metrics and analytics
  • Timestamp and log data

Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Operating system

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Platform Functionality: To provide and improve our services
  • Trading Operations: To execute and track transactions
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve user experience
  • Communication: To send important updates and notifications
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Public Blockchain Data

  • Wallet addresses and transaction data are publicly visible on the Solana blockchain
  • Vault performance metrics and trader rankings are publicly displayed

Service Providers

  • Privy: Authentication and wallet services
  • Jupiter: Trading execution and price aggregation
  • Railway: Platform hosting and infrastructure
  • Helius: Blockchain data and RPC services

Legal Requirements

  • When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • To prevent fraud or other illegal activities

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authentication
  • Monitoring: Continuous security monitoring and threat detection
  • Regular Audits: Regular security audits and penetration testing

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Blockchain Data

Please be aware that:

  • All blockchain transactions are publicly visible and permanently recorded
  • Your wallet address and transaction history can be analyzed by anyone
  • We cannot control or prevent third-party blockchain analysis
  • Pseudonymous does not mean anonymous - your identity may be linked to your wallet

6. X (Twitter) Integration

When you link your X (Twitter) account:

  • We receive your public X profile information (handle, display name, profile image)
  • We display your verification badge on the platform
  • We do not access your private messages or friends
  • You can revoke X access at any time

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in
  • Analytics: To understand how you use the platform
  • Performance: To optimize platform performance

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for:

  • Account Data: As long as your account remains active
  • Transaction Data: Permanently (required for blockchain operations)
  • Analytics Data: Up to 2 years
  • Legal Requirements: As required by applicable laws

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data (subject to blockchain requirements)
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of non-essential data collection
  • Export: Export your data in a portable format

10. Children's Privacy

Trader Vaults is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on the Platform and updating the "Last Updated" date.
