5. Vault Mechanics

5.1 Fee Structure

Profits from vaults are distributed as follows:

  • 70% to investors (depositors)
  • 30% to the trader
  • The Platform takes 0% directly from vault profits

One-third of the trader's fee is held in escrow for 60 days before becoming claimable.

5.2 Trader Limitations

Traders cannot withdraw funds from vaults they manage. Custody remains with the smart contract, and withdrawals are only available to investors who deposited funds.

5.3 High-Water Mark Protection

Each investor's fee calculations are based on their personal high-water mark, ensuring traders only earn performance fees on net new profits.
